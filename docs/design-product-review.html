Product and brand

Clean type, friendly guidance and honest eSIM education.

What we borrowed as principles from OneTrust and Airalo, what we rejected, and what changed in Omniroam.

Decision

Use the discipline, not the dress-up.

Omniroam keeps its own Australian animal crew and warm palette. The customer journey now uses a calmer type hierarchy, more breathing room and a stronger teaching sequence before purchase and installation.

Reference critique

OneTrust

Useful traits include confident sans-serif type, restrained navigation, generous white space, clear contrast and large sections with one job each. Its black-and-white corporate severity would make Omniroam feel less warm, so we borrowed the discipline without copying the visual identity.

Airalo

Airalo puts destination search, traveller situations, “How it works”, eSIM education, device help and FAQs close to the buying path. The people-led artwork adds context and movement. The risk for Omniroam is becoming another global marketplace with interchangeable illustrations. Our animal crew therefore remains the recognisable guide, while copy focuses on real traveller moments such as airport maps, rides, hotel details and home-number SMS.

What changed in the customer MVP

  • A dedicated explanation of data-only service now appears before the setup journey.
  • The three-step purchase story remains short, then expands into separate iPhone and Android installation tabs.
  • Each phone guide shows an original menu illustration and links to the manufacturer’s current screenshots.
  • The setup copy tells customers to install on reliable Wi-Fi before departure, label the line and switch mobile data after landing.
  • A visible warning tells customers not to delete an installed eSIM because many activation codes work once.

Data-only product rules

Most Omniroam plans support maps, browsing, rides, email and internet-based calls or messages. Unless a plan says otherwise, it does not include a local phone number, ordinary carrier calls or SMS.

  • Never imply that bank or government verification codes are guaranteed overseas.
  • Explain that customers may keep their Australian SIM installed, but their home carrier controls SMS availability and roaming charges.
  • Do not promise Wi-Fi calling or phone-number support unless the exact device, carrier and plan have been checked.
  • Show “data-only” before payment and on the private installation page.

Next checks

  1. Test the final wording on recent Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel versions because Android menu labels vary.
  2. Add installation status to the order page so reminders stop after successful setup.
  3. Create help screenshots from owned test devices when available. Do not copy competitors’ screenshots.
  4. Record support failures by device model and improve the guide using actual cases.
