One crew. Six reusable character masters.

Logo decision

Omniroam

The koala-in-pin badge was retired because its shield shape resembled a park or tourism-authority emblem at small sizes. The new mark is an abstract O and travel dot. It remains separate from Omi, so the character can change pose without weakening the logo.

What keeps the system consistent

Always

  • Deep-navy variable outline
  • Saturated colour and clean two-step shading
  • Large expressive eyes and a clear silhouette
  • Transparent background and complete body
  • One emotional job per placement

Never

  • Watercolour or paper texture
  • Washed-out beige scenes
  • Generic geometric mascot construction
  • Character art behind important copy
  • Humour around lost money or failed service
