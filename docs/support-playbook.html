Portal or API faults are internal.

Customers never choose a supplier issue. Engineering or Tier 3 staff use that route when Omniroam's record and the supplier's record disagree, a webhook is missing or an API call fails repeatedly.

Automation boundary

Tier 1 can provide compatibility checks, setup instructions, recovery guidance and top-up availability. Tier 2 can run structured activation and connectivity diagnostics. Tier 3 handles refunds, payment uncertainty, repeated provisioning failures, legal concerns and supplier disputes.

Automation may suggest the next check, collect safe details and draft a reply. It must not ask for card data, activation codes or full ICCIDs. It must not approve a refund or create another supplier order.

Still required before live sales

  • case assignment, notes and safe attachments

  • reviewed device-specific help articles
