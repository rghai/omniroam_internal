Compliance foundation

Privacy, consent and customer rights

What is implemented in the MVP, and what must happen before live sales.

This is a product foundation, not a legal sign-off.

The policies are drafted around Australian Privacy Principles, Australian Consumer Law and GDPR-style transparency and rights. A qualified review is still required once the legal operator, countries and processing arrangements are final.

Implemented

  • Readable privacy, terms, cookie, refund and price-comparison pages
  • Legal policy version and acceptance time stored with each order
  • Open-source CookieConsent v3 under the MIT licence
  • Google Consent Mode values set before optional tags can load
  • Opt-in, opt-out and notice-only configuration with per-category defaults
  • Testing configured as opt-out, with marketing storage denied
  • Separate optional permission for email, SMS, WhatsApp and LINE
  • Privacy-request form, reference, identity-pending state, audit trail and 30-day internal target
  • Human-only refunds and preserved Australian Consumer Law rights
  • Automatic expiry for dated competitor claims

DSAR workflow

  1. Record the request without revealing whether the email exists.
  2. Verify identity with a private, time-limited email link.
  3. Search order, payment metadata, eSIM state, recovery, refunds, support, email, marketing consent and analytics systems.
  4. Redact secrets, activation codes and information about other people.
  5. Approve, partly approve or refuse with a recorded lawful reason.
  6. Deliver exports through an expiring private link and record completion.

Still required

  • Legal entity name, address, jurisdiction and registration details
  • External legal review
  • Identity-verification email and authenticated founder case queue
  • Processor-specific export and deletion adapters
  • Consent withdrawal centre and channel suppression adapters
  • Overdue privacy-case alerts
  • Final production cookie regime
  • Data-retention schedule and deletion jobs
  • Data breach response drill
