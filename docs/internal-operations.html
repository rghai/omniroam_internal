Internal operations

How privacy and support work stays private.

The public founder board tracks readiness. Customer cases belong in a separate staff system with its own sign-in.

The boundary

Customer access is not staff access.

An order link, future customer account or marketing identity can never open the operations dashboard. Staff tokens must come from a separate issuer, carry the expected audience, use a valid signature and match an approved staff domain and role.

Plumbing now in place

Case records

  • Support ticket, topic, priority and support tier
  • First-response and resolution deadlines
  • Append-only support event history
  • DSAR identity status, due date and audit trail

Monitoring records

  • Open and overdue queue counts
  • One durable alert per case and reason
  • Separate staff identity and role records
  • Protected sweep endpoint for a future scheduler

Still placeholders

  1. Staff identity provider, allowed email domain and role assignments
  2. Monitored support and privacy inboxes
  3. Alert delivery destination, retries and acknowledgement
  4. Secure DSAR identity verification and document delivery
  5. Case assignment, notes, replies and attachments
  6. Named Tier 3, privacy and refund owners
