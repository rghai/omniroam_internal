Market scorecard · 31 August 2026

The numbers do not prove Australia is the biggest or best market.

They support Australia as a focused acquisition test while the product stays global.

Decision

Open founder decision

Use Australia as the first test, not as a permanent company identity. Keep a neutral global version and compare measured results before committing paid acquisition or a country-specific rename.

Recommended routes: /au/en and /global/en. Use paths rather than subdomains. Suggest a market from location, never force it. Save the selected market and language on the order so checkout, legal copy and email remain consistent.

Why test Australia first anyway?

Enough demand

12.7 million short trips, 60.8% for holidays and a 14-day median trip give a useful audience and a clear whole-trip roaming comparison.

Cheaper to learn

Australian English, AUD and founders who can judge the local copy reduce launch execution risk.

Useful destination mix

Indonesia, Japan, Thailand and Vietnam combine strong Australian traffic with workable wholesale packages.

Not yet proven commercially

Acquisition cost, conversion, support effort and actual margin still need a live controlled test.

How founders close the decision

Compare an Australian campaign with a neutral global-English control and one second English market. Approve thresholds for conversion, customer acquisition cost, gross margin, installation success, support contacts, refunds and chargebacks before buying traffic.

Recommendation

Australia remains the priority only if it clears the agreed thresholds. If it does not, change the market.
