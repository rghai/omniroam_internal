Non-negotiable rules

  • No secret, QR, activation value, customer data or private token in GitHub, Notion or public evidence.
  • No paid service or supplier spend without founder approval.
  • One successful payment creates no more than one supplier order.
  • Refund approval is human-only.
  • Customers never choose or see the supplier.
  • Every release runs the automated, security and visual gates.
  • Customer and founder copy uses Australian English and no em dashes.
