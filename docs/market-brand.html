Decision

Start with Australian leisure travellers.

Focus the first 90 days on 7–21 day trips, lead with Japan, add USA second, and retain Indonesia/Bali, Thailand, Vietnam and Europe. Treat South America as search-led and Africa as coverage-QA-first. Review Australia after 100 paid orders or 90 days.

This is a launch wedge, not a geographic limit. The product remains globally extensible.

Why Australia

Enough demand

ABS recorded 12,711,650 short-term resident returns in 2025–26. Holidays were 60.8%, implying about 7.73m holiday trips. A 100-order validation target is only about 0.0013% of that annual estimate.

Strong catalogue fit

Indonesia, Japan, Thailand and Vietnam together accounted for about 3.96m trips. Japan alone recorded 992,550 trips and the median Japan trip was 15 days.

Clear price pain

A 13-day median holiday can imply A$65–A$130 in A$5–A$10/day roaming charges. A whole-trip eSIM can be explained simply, with honest inclusions caveats.

Operational fit

AUD retail, English-first service and an already-tested payment path reduce the number of unknowns in the first test.

How the figures are calculated

RBA 28 Aug 2026 reference: A$1 = US$0.7196, therefore US$1 ≈ A$1.3897. The wholesale A$ column retains the current supplier-model rounding. Markup = (retail − wholesale A$) ÷ wholesale A$. Product gross margin = (retail − wholesale A$) ÷ retail.

Product GM excludes payment fees/FX, GST/tax, refunds, chargebacks, support, promotions, affiliates and customer acquisition. Contribution margin will be lower. Reprice using actual settlement FX plus a buffer before launch.

Competitive position

There is no single competitor price.

We must compare three baskets separately: Australian home-carrier roaming for maximum continuity, global travel eSIMs for like-for-like digital purchase, and destination-local carriers for the lowest price floor. Mixing them would overstate our advantage.

Validate exact included countries before publishing savings claims.

3. Australian home-carrier roaming

Telstra’s A$10/day and eligible Optus/Vodafone A$5/day offers may include calls, texts, home-number continuity and more data. Over a 14-day trip that is an A$70–A$140 convenience anchor, but it is a different product. Keep it lower on the sales page and state the inclusions.

Brand recommendation

  • Lead with fair, clear, whole-trip value, not “cheap data.”
  • Hero proof: “Japan 5GB for A$12.99 — one clear price for the trip.”
  • For regional plans, lead with one eSIM across multiple countries—not a blanket cheapest-price claim.
  • Omi is the visible setup guide and support voice, never presented as a human.
  • Review three complete directions: Signal Friendly, Wayfinder and Clear Sky.
  • A formal rename requires domain, trademark, pronunciation and language screening.
