Market decision

Australia is the first test because the numbers line up

A measured launch wedge—not a forever decision.

Australia combines 7.73m estimated annual holiday trips, unusually strong demand for our low-cost Asian catalogue, a 13-day median holiday and a simple A$5–A$10-per-day carrier-roaming price anchor. Revisit after 100 paid orders or 90 days.
