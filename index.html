Market decision

Australia is evidence-backed, but not hard-coded.

The case for Australia

Australians recorded 12.7 million short overseas trips in 2025–26. Australia ranked sixth worldwide for outbound tourism spend at US$46 billion in 2024. The median trip was 14 days, which exposes the whole-trip cost of daily roaming.

The case for keeping options open

China leads global outbound spend, while the USA, Germany, UK, France and India are major source markets. Their scale is real. Each adds payment, language, support, compliance and acquisition work that must be measured against order value.

Where everything lives

One product, several tools with distinct jobs

1

GitHub

Version history and source of truth. omniroam holds the customer app. omniroam_internal holds this public, non-secret briefing.

2

Vercel

Builds and runs the public customer product and server routes. A GitHub push triggers deployment. Vercel is not the permanent order database.

3

Neon

Postgres database for orders, payments, eSIM state, consent, support, privacy cases and alerts. It remembers what Vercel processes.

4

Resend

Delivers first-party transactional email. Templates live in our source. eSIMAccess does not control customer-facing email.

5

Notion

Private working tracker for owners, due dates, decisions and granular tasks. It can include founder-only operational notes, but never secrets.

6

Founder dashboard

This branded GitHub Pages site summarises evidence and links to detail. It stays safe to share because it contains no credentials or customer records.

7

Opn

Payment gateway. It accepts the customer charge and settles to the Thai account. FX from AUD presentment to THB settlement remains a revenue-ops item.

8

eSIMAccess and future suppliers

Supply data packages and eSIM state behind a private provider registry. Customers never choose or see the supplier.

Launch sequence

The path to a 15 September decision

30 AUGUST TO 2 SEPTEMBER

Close the safety foundation

Migrate security tables, configure encryption, finish one-use recovery testing, define fraud holds, connect monitored inboxes and decide the legal operator.

3 TO 6 SEPTEMBER

Prove the complete purchase

Pass 3-D Secure, verified webhooks and reconciliation. Run one cheap supplier-order canary only after the guardrails pass. Confirm branded email in a real inbox.

7 TO 10 SEPTEMBER

Rehearse operations

Run support, recovery, failed payment, missing eSIM, refund, fraud and privacy drills. Fix every release-blocking finding.

11 TO 13 SEPTEMBER

Limited founder and invited-user pilot

Use a strict spend cap, low order volume and human review. Measure conversion, setup success, support demand and real margin.

14 SEPTEMBER

Go or no-go review

Review every P0 gate, legal sign-off, on-call coverage, rollback, supplier balance and unresolved high-risk issue.

15 SEPTEMBER

Controlled launch only if every P0 gate is green

No deadline can override payment safety, customer delivery, legal readiness or human incident ownership.
