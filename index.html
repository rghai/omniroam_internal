Market decision

Australia does not win the raw-volume comparison.

The official numbers

Australia recorded 12.7 million short overseas trips. The latest suitable official releases show 114.0 million for Germany, 107.7 million for the USA, 94.6 million for the UK, 43.3 million for Canada, 30.9 million for India and 14.7 million for Japan. Definitions differ, but the scale result is clear.

Why it still deserves a test

Australian English, AUD and founder familiarity make the first campaign easier to judge and support. That is an execution advantage, not proof of market superiority. A global-English control prevents us from confusing local confidence with customer demand.

Localisation decision

Global product, path-based country experiences.

/global/en

Neutral English, broad destination coverage and no assumption about the traveller's home country.

/au/en

Australian campaign copy, AUD base pricing, local comparisons and Australian support language.

Later markets

Add paths such as `/gb/en`. IP may suggest the right path but must not force it. Store the selected market and locale on the order so email uses the purchase context.

This approach avoids subdomain and translation sprawl while keeping country-specific content measurable. Local pages need named owners, review dates and claim expiry rules.
