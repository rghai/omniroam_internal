Market decision

Australia is the first test because the numbers line up

A measured launch wedge—not a forever decision.

Australia combines 7.73m estimated annual holiday trips, unusually strong demand for our low-cost Asian catalogue, a 13-day median holiday and a simple A$5–A$10-per-day carrier-roaming price anchor. Revisit after 100 paid orders or 90 days.

Three different competitors now shape the decision.

Australian roaming passes are the expensive convenience anchor. Global travel-eSIM sellers are our closest like-for-like competitors. Local carriers set the lowest price floor but usually trade cross-border convenience for a local SIM/account and one-country coverage. Japan and USA remain price-competitive at the revised prices; South America and Africa must be positioned as regional convenience, not the cheapest data.
